- Electrical: Tesla Coil towers and the Quantum Entangler, wires in six metals, batteries, charger, transformer, multimeter,
  solar panel, thermopile, Electromagnetic Levitator, Quantum Glyphs and Gates, Mining Laser.
- Mechanical: gears, shafts, Hand Crank, wind and water turbines, Electric Motor.
- Ore processing and smelting: Mechanical Piston, Grinding Wheel, Millstone, Mixer, Filter, dusts for every metal, Firebox,
  Hot Plate, Casting Mold and molten metal.
- Fluids: gutters, tanks, six pipes, pump and grate on the original's pressure model.
- Workshop and logistics: crates, Engineering Table, Hammer, Imprinter, Turntable, conveyor belts, manipulators, detectors,
  sorters, breakers and placers.
- Atomic: uranium and radiation, Chemical Extractor, Nuclear Boiler, Centrifuge, fission reactors with electric turbines,
  thermometers and sirens, the Particle Accelerator, antimatter and dark matter, Fulmination Generator, Quantum Assembler,
  Plasma Heater and fusion, and the Creative Builder with the original's schematics.
- Feature switches in the common config; balance numbers in the server config; JEI descriptions for everything.
