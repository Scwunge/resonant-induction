# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)
- First 1.21.1 NeoForge release.
- Tesla Coil: stackable towers that beam Forge Energy to other towers, frequency colours, receive and mob-zap toggles,
  redstone off switch, Quantum Entangler links (also across dimensions).
- Electromagnetic Levitator: pull/push item beams between levitators, linked pathfinding around corners.
- Quantum Glyphs and Quantum Gates: frequency gates that teleport entities and share an item slot and a tank.
- Charger and Mining Laser (remove, smelt and damage modes).
- Feature switches in the common config to drop recipes for parts another mod already covers.
